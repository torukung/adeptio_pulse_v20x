#Field TypeMeaning

Templated, never raw

route_template carries the template, so cardinality stays bounded and two menus grouping by route can never disagree. A raw URI would make the drill-down grid unbuildable.

Two codes, two columns

status is what the client saw; result_code is the domain outcome the application emitted. They stay separate, which is the only reason a business decline carried on HTTP 200 is visible at all.

Callee time, not total

upstream_ms is the callee's own time and dur_ms the whole hop. Downstream Health quotes the first; SLA quotes the second. One field mixed up moves every latency figure on the site.

Every assertion above runs against a seeded account and asserts on the body, not the status line. That is what separates “the API answered” from “the customer got what they asked for” — and it is the whole reason the silent-decline window is visible on this site.

A watermark is the point up to which a node has proved it shipped everything. Buckets after it are unknown, not zero — every aggregate on this site drops them from the denominator instead of averaging a nought into the result, which is why the affected cells read provisional and never 0 %.
